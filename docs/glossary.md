# DAWLYY glossary: training and nutrition terms

Reference for message catalogs (ar / en). Arabic is Egyptian as coaches say it; "Franco" is how coaches type it in WhatsApp. One-line meanings are for translators, not for end users.

| Arabic (Egyptian) | English | Franco | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| تمرين | Exercise | tamreen | A single movement, such as a squat or a row. |
| جلسة تمرين | Workout / session | gelset tamreen | One training day from warm-up to cool-down. |
| برنامج | Program | bernameg | The full plan of weeks and workouts a client follows. |
| مجموعة | Set | magmo3a / set | A group of consecutive reps without rest. |
| تكرار | Rep (repetition) | tekrar / rep | One complete movement of an exercise. |
| راحة بين المجموعات | Rest between sets | rahet bein el magmo3at | The time you wait before the next set. |
| إحماء | Warm-up | ehmaa / warm up | Light preparation before the main work. |
| تهدئة | Cool-down | tahdea / cool down | Easy movement and stretching after training. |
| وزن | Weight / load | wazn | The resistance lifted in a set. |
| وزن الجسم | Bodyweight | wazn el gesm | Training with only your own weight as resistance. |
| أقصى تكرار واحد | One-rep max (1RM) | aqsa tekrar wahed / 1RM | The heaviest weight you can lift once with good form. |
| شدة | Intensity | shedda | How heavy or hard the work is relative to your max. |
| حجم التمرين | Training volume | 7agm el tamreen | Total sets and reps done for a muscle in a week. |
| تكرارات في الاحتياطي | Reps in reserve (RIR) | tekrarat fel ehtiyaty / RIR | How many more reps you could have done. |
| معدل الجهد المدرك | Rate of perceived exertion (RPE) | RPE | A 1 to 10 score for how hard a set felt. |
| فشل عضلي | Muscle failure | fashal 3adaly | The point where you cannot complete another rep. |
| زيادة تدريجية | Progressive overload | zyada tadrigeya | Slowly increasing weight, reps or sets over time. |
| أسبوع تخفيف | Deload week | usboo3 takhfeef / deload | A lighter week to recover and keep progressing. |
| مرحلة | Phase / block | marhala | A set of weeks with one training goal. |
| تمارين مركبة | Compound exercises | tamareen morakkaba | Moves that work several joints and muscles. |
| تمارين عزل | Isolation exercises | tamareen 3azl | Moves that target one muscle and one joint. |
| سوبر ست | Superset | super set | Two exercises done back to back with no rest. |
| دروب ست | Drop set | drop set | Reducing the weight right after failure and continuing. |
| سيركت | Circuit | circuit / sercit | Several exercises done in a round with little rest. |
| تمرين ثابت | Isometric hold | tamreen sabet | Holding a position without moving, like a plank. |
| إيقاع الحركة | Tempo | eqa3 el 7araka / tempo | The speed of lowering, pausing and lifting. |
| مدى الحركة | Range of motion | mada el 7araka / ROM | How far a joint moves in the exercise. |
| وضعية | Form / posture | wad3eya / form | The body position while performing a movement. |
| ثبات الجذع | Core stability | sabat el gez3 | Keeping your trunk firm during movement. |
| عضلات الجذع | Core | core / 3adalat el gez3 | The muscles around the abdomen, back and hips. |
| عضلة الصدر | Chest (pectorals) | 3adalet el sadr / chest | The main pushing muscle at the front of the torso. |
| عضلة الظهر | Back (lats) | 3adalet el dahr / back | The pulling muscles along the back and sides. |
| الأكتاف | Shoulders (deltoids) | el aktaf / shoulders | The rounded muscles on top of the arms. |
| الباي | Biceps | bayseps / bice | The muscle at the front of the upper arm. |
| التراي | Triceps | tray / triceps | The muscle at the back of the upper arm. |
| الفخد الأمامي | Quadriceps | el fakhd el amamy / quads | The large muscle at the front of the thigh. |
| الفخد الخلفي | Hamstrings | el fakhd el khalfy / hamstring | The muscles at the back of the thigh. |
| الجلوت | Glutes | glutes / el gloot | The buttock muscles that extend the hip. |
| السمانة | Calves | el somana / calves | The muscles at the back of the lower leg. |
| بطن | Abs | batn / abs | The muscles at the front of the abdomen. |
| ترابيس | Trapezius (traps) | trabis / traps | The upper back and neck muscles that shrug. |
| بنش بريس | Bench press | bench press / bens | Pressing a bar from the chest while lying on a bench. |
| سكوات | Squat | squat / skwat | Bending hips and knees to lower, then standing up. |
| ديد ليفت | Deadlift | deadlift / dead lift | Lifting a bar from the floor to hip height. |
| عقلة | Pull-up | o2la / pull up | Hanging from a bar and pulling your chin over it. |
| ضغط | Push-up | daght / push up | Pressing your body off the floor with arms. |
| لانجز | Lunge | lunges / lanjez | A stepping move that lowers one knee toward the floor. |
| بلانك | Plank | plank | A straight-body hold on forearms or hands. |
| تمرين كارديو | Cardio | cardio / kardyo | Training that raises heart rate for a longer time. |
| مشاية | Treadmill | mashaya / treadmill | A machine for walking or running in place. |
| مرونة | Mobility / flexibility | moroona / mobility | How well joints and muscles move through range. |
| تمدد | Stretch | tamaddod / stretching | Lengthening a muscle and holding the position. |
| إصابة | Injury | esaba | Damage to a muscle, joint or tendon. |
| ألم | Pain | alam | An uncomfortable signal; sharp pain means stop. |
| تعافي | Recovery | ta3afy / recovery | Rest, sleep and food that let the body adapt. |
| نوم | Sleep | nom | Night rest, the main driver of recovery. |
| سعرات حرارية | Calories (kcal) | so3rat / calories | The unit of energy in food. |
| ماكروز | Macros | macros | Protein, carbs and fat: the three energy nutrients. |
| بروتين | Protein | protein / protein | The nutrient that builds and repairs muscle. |
| كربوهيدرات | Carbohydrates | carbs / karbohydrate | The main fuel for training and the brain. |
| دهون | Fats | dohoon / fats | The dense energy source that supports hormones. |
| ألياف | Fiber | alyaf / fiber | Plant material that supports digestion and fullness. |
| سكر | Sugar | sokkar / sugar | Simple carbohydrates that digest quickly. |
| ميكروز / فيتامينات ومعادن | Micronutrients | micros / vitamins | Vitamins and minerals needed in small amounts. |
| مياه | Water / hydration | mayya / hydration | Fluid intake to support performance and health. |
| وجبة | Meal | wagba / meal | A planned eating occasion. |
| وجبة خفيفة | Snack | snack / wagba khafifa | A small meal between main meals. |
| وجبة حرة | Free meal / cheat meal | free meal / cheat meal | A planned meal outside the plan, not a failure. |
| خطة غذائية | Meal plan | khetta ghazaeya / meal plan | A day-by-day plan of foods and portions. |
| عجز سعرات | Calorie deficit | agz so3rat / deficit | Eating below maintenance to lose weight. |
| فائض سعرات | Calorie surplus | fa2ed so3rat / surplus | Eating above maintenance to gain weight. |
| سعرات الثبات | Maintenance calories | so3rat el sabat / maintenance | The intake that keeps weight stable. |
| معدل الحرق الأساسي | Basal metabolic rate (BMR) | BMR | Energy your body burns at rest. |
| إجمالي الحرق اليومي | Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) | TDEE | BMR plus activity: your total daily burn. |
| تضخيم | Bulking | tadkheem / bulk | A phase of eating more to build muscle. |
| تنشيف | Cutting | tansheef / cut | A phase of eating less to lose fat. |
| إعادة تكوين الجسم | Body recomposition | recomp | Losing fat and gaining muscle at the same time. |
| ملعقة كبيرة | Tablespoon | ma3la2a kebeera / tbsp | A measure, treated here as 15 g. |
| ملعقة صغيرة | Teaspoon | ma3la2a sogayara / tsp | A measure, treated here as 5 g. |
| كوب | Cup | koob / cup | A measure of 240 ml. |
| رغيف بلدي | Baladi loaf | raghif baladi | An Egyptian whole wheat flatbread, about 90 g. |
| رغيف فينو | Fino roll | raghif fino | A small white bread roll, about 60 g. |
| كف يد | Palm / handful portion | kaff eed | A hand-based portion for foods without a scale. |
| نيء | Raw | nay | The state before cooking; weigh it as raw. |
| مطبوخ | Cooked | matbookh | The state after cooking; weigh it as cooked. |
| مكمل غذائي | Supplement | mokammel / supplement | A product that adds nutrients to the diet. |
| واي بروتين | Whey protein | whey / wai protein | A fast-digesting milk protein powder. |
| كرياتين | Creatine | creatine / kreatin | A supplement that supports strength and power. |
| وزن مثالي | Target weight | wazn mesaly | The body weight a client is working toward. |
| قياسات الجسم | Body measurements | qeyasat el gesm | Waist, hips, chest and other circumference readings. |
| نسبة الدهون | Body fat percentage | nesbet el dohoon / BF% | The share of body weight that is fat. |
| متابعة أسبوعية | Weekly check-in | mota6a3a osbo3eya / check in | A weekly report on weight, photos and adherence. |
| التزام | Adherence | eltizam | How closely the client follows the plan. |
