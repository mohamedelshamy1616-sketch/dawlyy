# FitXpert (FITXPERT) as a coach-business platform benchmark for DAWLYY, state as of 7 Nov 2026

Method note: fitxpert.team and fitexpert.team are JS-rendered; WebFetch returned only a title. Content below was read via Exa fetch/search of the rendered pages. G2 and Crunchbase block WebFetch (403); G2 was read via Exa, Crunchbase could not be read. About 11 tool calls used. No Google Play review text, no app screenshots and no in-product trial were inspected.

## Company: who, where, when, team, funding, scale

### Takeaway
FitXpert is an Egyptian-built SaaS (Mansoura dev house Codebase; founders Mostafa Mahmoud and Salah Selim/Seleem) with a London registered address, launched 2023-2024, with a seven-figure (currency undisclosed) round from Foras Investment announced 6 Jan 2026. Scale claims (150,000+ clients, 400-1000+ coaches) are self-reported and inconsistent across sources.

### Cited Findings
- Founded 2023 by Salah Selim and Mostafa Mahmoud; seven-figure strategic investment from Foras Investment (owner Mohamed Abou El-Naga Nagaty) under the "0107 Invest" initiative; funds go to tech stack, operations and Gulf expansion. Currency and exact amount not disclosed. — [Wamda, 6 Jan 2026](https://www.wamda.com/en/2026/01/fitxpert-secures-seven-figure-backing-scale-fitness-nutrition-saas-platform); also [EgyptInnovate](https://egyptinnovate.com/en/news/mohamed-abou-el-naga-invests-in-fitxpert-a-saas-solutions-provider-for-the-training-and-nutrition)
- Positions itself as "infrastructure rather than a point solution" for trainers, nutrition centres and clinics across the Arab world. — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2026/01/fitxpert-secures-seven-figure-backing-scale-fitness-nutrition-saas-platform)
- Origin story: built as an internal platform for the founder's own coaching business serving thousands of clients, then productised. Founder says it serves "more than 150,000 clients" across MENA. — [Mostafa Mahmoud LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/mostafamahmoud43400)
- Founder Mahmoud (b. 1997) also runs Codebase (in-house dev, Laravel/Vue.js, Mansoura Egypt), iTrainer (consumer fitness app, claimed 600,000+ users), Circles Association, Enrich Academy. IssueWire press piece (self-published pressroom) says FitXpert launched 2024, "400+ coaches managing 150,000+ clients", registered in London, expanding to Gulf and Europe. — [IssueWire, 1 May 2025](https://www.issuewire.com/mostafa-mahmoud-the-young-entrepreneur-redefining-healthtech-and-fitness-in-the-mena-region-1830782216764459)
- Salah Selim posted in Aug 2024 that FitXpert is "the first SaaS for this sector in the Arab world", with branded apps, an affiliate/"Partnership Hub" commission programme and claimed app-driven sales/retention uplift of "at least 30%". — [Salah Seleem LinkedIn](https://ae.linkedin.com/posts/salahseleem_fitxpert-%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%B9%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A7-activity-7228041305870786560--Mv_)

- Website lists contact "71 Shelton ST, Covent Garden, London, UK", email info@fitexpert.team (note spelling "fitexpert") and an Egyptian phone number. — [fitxpert.team](https://fitxpert.team/); [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)

### Inferences
- London address is likely a registered/virtual office; real operations are Egypt (and UAE per founder LinkedIn). Not verified.
- Coach count is soft: 400+ (May 2025 press) vs 1000+ (landing page) vs 4.4-star/5K+ Play downloads. 5K+ downloads of the shared client app suggests the paying coach base is in the hundreds, not thousands, unless branded apps are separate listings.
- 150,000 "clients" likely includes clients of the founder's own coaching business and cumulative records, not active paid subscribers.

### Gaps
- Exact round size/currency, headcount (LinkedIn 7 vs Codebase 20-30 shared staff), revenue, churn: not found. Crunchbase unreadable (403/timeout).

## Coach-side features

### Takeaway
Feature breadth is solid for the Egyptian coach workflow: client profiles, subscriptions, lead management, workout and diet builders with 1000+ video exercises and a thousand-plus food items, check-in forms, chat, reminders, challenges, team roles, reports, branded app. Payments gateways and white-label depth are claimed vaguely.

### Cited Findings
- Homepage pillars: Client Engagement (diet plan, workout programs, progress sharing); Client Management (subscription management, client profile, leads management); Business Growth (unlimited co-workers, custom branding, reports). — [fitexpert.team via Exa](https://fitexpert.team/)
- Arabic landing: central per-client dashboard; smart check-in data-collection form; automatic reminders and chat; 1000+ exercise videos with ability to add own exercises; built-in food database (1000+ items); client mobile app; progress photo comparison; automated invoices and renewals; motivation/challenge system. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Program builder supports supersets, drop sets, circuits; auto workout logging; weight log; smart rest timer. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Nutrition: automatic macro/calorie calculation; instant alerts when a client logs data; clients can request diet-plan changes in app. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Client management: auto reminders for follow-up/invoice/renewal; full profile with notes and medical history; follow-up report generation; filters and tags by goal/programme/status. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Business intelligence: renewal rates, adherence rates, programme performance; subscription management per package; team management with per-member permissions; commission/affiliate programmes. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Challenges: coach designs challenges from dashboard, picks scoring method and prizes. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- "Branded app": app under coach's name and logo on App Store and Google Play, colours and identity; push notifications; coach can send questionnaires and offers. — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Coach-facing pages hosted on codebase-product.com (example coach "Ziad") show per-coach public pages with packages sold via InstaPay, Visa and Fawry, manual WhatsApp follow-up and bank-transfer confirmation within 24 working hours. — [example coach page](https://ziad-mm.codebase-product.com/transformations/1)
- G2 reviewer praise: four plan categories (workout, fitness, nutrition, mobility), diet calculators for loss/gain/maintenance, client management. G2 summary notes "occasional technical issues". — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/fitxpert)

### Inferences
- The Ziad page suggests payments run through InstaPay/Visa/Fawry on a coach-hosted sales page, with manual confirmation. That implies no fully automatic Egyptian gateway reconciliation for at least that setup. Single example only.
- Intake forms and lead management exist but depth (funnels, WhatsApp integration, lead pipeline stages) is unknown.
- Offering "1000+" exercises and food items is modest next to global competitors; likely Arabic-localised content is the draw.

### Gaps
- No product demo or help centre read; no confirmation of Paymob, Stripe, Vodafone Cash integration; no white-label domain details; no video/live-call features; no habit tracking, wearables, or AI features found.

- Release history, App Store: 5.3.0 (19 Dec 2024), 6.3.0 (12 Jan 2025), 6.6/6.7 (17 Feb 2025), 7.5.0 (28 Apr 2025), 8.8.0 (28 May 2025), 9.0.0 (20 Jun 2025), 9.1.x Aug to Nov 2025, 9.2.0 to 9.5.0 on dates in 2026 listed without year (9.5.0 on 21 Sept). ## Pricing and payments

### Takeaway
Freemium with client-count tiers, USD-denominated on G2: Free for 3 clients, paid from $19/month up to $80 for 10 to 250 clients; corporate tier is custom. 14-day free trial, no card.

### Cited Findings
- G2 (vendor-provided): Free "Grow" plan with 3 clients; "Individual 10 to 250 clients, $19 to $80 monthly" starting at $19 for 10 clients. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/fitxpert)
- Website tiers: Free (1 team member, 3 clients, 6 plans/month, food and workout library, client data management, reports, custom branding, flexible subscriptions); Individual (3 team members, plans builder, same libraries, team scalability); Corporate (unlimited team members). Prices not shown in the fetched text. — [fitexpert.team via Exa](https://fitexpert.team/)
- Landing page: "14-day free trial, no credit card required". — [landing.fitxpert.team](https://landing.fitxpert.team/)
- Custom branding is listed even on the Free tier on the website. — [fitexpert.team via Exa](https://fitexpert.team/)

### Inferences
- Pricing is per-client-band flat subscription; no revenue share or setup fee seen, but absence is not proof.
- Whether the EGP price differs from USD G2 listing is unknown.

### Gaps
- Official current price table, EGP pricing, setup fees for branded app publishing, and payment gateways inside the coach product (Paymob, Fawry, Stripe, Vodafone Cash, InstaPay) not confirmed beyond the one coach page above.

## Where DAWLYY must match
- Arabic-first client app plus coach dashboard; Egyptian dialect copy.
- Diet builder with automatic macros and a food database; exercise library 1000+ with video; custom exercises; superset and circuit programming.
- Check-in forms, progress photos, measurements, weight log, rest timer, workout logging.
- Subscriptions, renewals, auto invoices and reminders; team roles with permissions; reports on renewals and adherence; challenges.
- A free tier (3 clients) and a 14-day no-card trial; client-band pricing in the roughly $19 to $80 range for up to 250 clients.
- Rapid release cadence and push notifications.

## Where DAWLYY can clearly beat it
- Real Egyptian payment automation (Paymob, Fawry, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash) with automatic reconciliation, instead of manual transfer confirmation.
- True white-label: own store listing, domain and colours per coach, not a shared "FITXPERT" app (verify first).
- Higher-quality Arabic food database (local dishes, portions) and larger exercise library.
- AI assistance (plan drafting, check-in summaries), WhatsApp-native lead capture and automation, video calls.
- Polish and reliability: avoid regressions like the weight-decimal bug; clear single domain and brand; transparent public pricing page and EGP pricing.
- Verified social proof: named independent coaches and case studies, which FitXpert lacks publicly.
