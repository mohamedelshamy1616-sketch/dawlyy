# Global online-coaching platforms as benchmarks for DAWLYY (state as of 2026-11-07)

Method note: about 21 tool calls (WebSearch snippets plus WebFetch of vendor pages). Most third-party sources are competitor blogs or affiliates (FitBudd, Coachway, QuickCoach, Trainerfu, Promealplan, Gymkee, PT Distinction), so they are biased. Vendor pages (trainerize.com, everfit.io, ABC Fitness blog) are primary for their own prices and features. Direct Reddit threads, Facebook groups, G2 pages and App Store review text were NOT retrievable. Treat complaint evidence as indicative, not measured.

## 1. Pricing tiers as of 2026 (per-client vs flat, white-label cost)

### Takeaway
Per-client, tiered pricing with paid add-ons is the norm (Trainerize, Everfit, TrueCoach, CoachRx, Kahunas per-seat). Flat unlimited-client pricing exists mainly at My PT Hub and PT Distinction. Full white-label (own App Store listing) is gated to top tiers or paid add-ons everywhere. Sources conflict on many figures.

### Cited Findings
- ABC Trainerize cards: Basic free (1 client); Grow $9/mo (2 clients, includes AI Workout Builder, meal/macro tracking); Pro 5 $23/mo scaling to 200 clients; Studio Plus $248/mo per location (500-1,000+ clients); annual saves 10%; 30-day trial — [trainerize.com/pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/)
- Trainerize add-ons: Advanced Nutrition $20/mo (small tiers) or $45/mo (30-200 clients); Business $25; Video Coaching $10; Stripe payments $10; FitMetrics from $6/client; Custom Branded App $169 one-time on Pro; Studio Plus includes branded app; $99/yr Apple Developer account paid separately — [trainerize.com/pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/)
- The same Trainerize page contradicts itself: FAQ lists Grow $10, Pro $25 (5 clients), Pro $135 (50 clients), Studio Plus $275 — [trainerize.com/pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/)
- Everfit: Starter free (5 clients); Pro from $19/mo ($16 annual) for 5 clients up to 300; Studio from $105/mo (50 clients) up to 500; Enterprise 500+ custom — [everfit.io/pricing](https://everfit.io/pricing/)
- Everfit add-ons: Autoflow from $29/mo (charged per client with an active flow); On-Demand $25; Payments & Packages $9; Meal Plans & Recipe Books $39/mo — [everfit.io/pricing](https://everfit.io/pricing/)
- Everfit white-label app is listed under Enterprise only (500+ clients); Pro and Studio get logo/colour branding; Enterprise includes Apple/Google developer support — [everfit.io/pricing](https://everfit.io/pricing/)
- TrueCoach (competitor-reported, inconsistent): $20/mo (5 clients), $53 (20), $107 (50); no free tier; no published pricing above 50 clients; payments only in US, UK, AU, CA — [FitBudd comparison](https://www.fitbudd.com/insights/everfit-vs-trainerize-vs-truecoach?c84a98fb_page=10), via search summary. One other source gave $136.99 for 50 clients. Treat as unverified.
- Kahunas (Coachway, checked 4 Nov 2026): per-seat $5/seat (3-24 seats, $15 minimum) falling to $2.50 (25-49), $2.00 (50-99), $1.50 (100-199), $1.00 (200-999); yearly 20% off; white-label app only from 200 seats (about $200/mo or more); 14-day trial — [Coachway Kahunas pricing](https://coachway.io/articles/kahunas-pricing/). Contradicts an earlier guide listing flat $35/$69/$99 tiers; Capterra lists "from $35" — [Capterra Kahunas](https://www.capterra.com/p/10028886/Kahunas/pricing/). The pricing model appears to have changed, or sources are stale.
- CoachRx (Capterra, updated March 2026): 1-5 clients $25/mo; 6-50 $67/mo; 51-150 $169/mo; no free version — [Capterra CoachRx](https://www.capterra.com/p/253158/CoachRx/pricing/)
- PT Distinction (Capterra): Basic $19.90/mo flat, Pro $59.90/mo flat; branded app gated to Pro/Master and is in-app branding, not an independent store listing (QuickCoach/FitBudd) — [Capterra PT Distinction](https://www.capterra.com/p/141155/PT-Distinction/pricing/)
- My PT Hub: Premium reported $52-$59/mo unlimited clients; white-label App Store listing reported as $145 one-time (QuickCoach) or a $145/mo add-on bringing the total to about $204/mo (FitBudd) — [FitBudd white-label pricing](https://www.fitbudd.com/insights/white-label-fitness-app-pricing-what-you-actually-pay); sources disagree.
- Fitr: pricing not retrieved. Hupe/Harder: see section 6 Gaps.

### Inferences
- A solo coach with 30-50 clients pays roughly $50-$150/mo before add-ons on per-client platforms; nutrition, automation and payments each add $9-$45/mo. Add-on stacking is the pricing pain point.
- Coaches who want their own App Store icon pay a lot (hundreds of dollars per month, or 200+ seats). A white-label offered at an affordable flat price is a differentiation lever.
- No platform's public pricing is in SAR/AED/EGP; Kahunas is USD-only (Coachway).

### Gaps
- Exact current TrueCoach, PT Distinction, My PT Hub and Fitr prices from vendor pages: not retrieved.
- Trainerize 2026 price change: one competitor blog claims +10% from 16 Mar 2026 ([harley-trainer blog](https://harley-trainer.pages.dev/blog/trainerize-price-increase-2026)); CostBench logged a 0.8% cut to $248 in May 2026 ([CostBench](https://costbench.com/changelog/trainerize-price-decrease-2026-05/)). Unresolved.

## 2. Feature depth (program builder, nutrition, check-ins, habits, messaging, photos, PDF, automation, roles, integrations, AI)

### Takeaway
Workout programming is mature everywhere; nutrition is the weakest layer (paid add-ons, manual logging, MyFitnessPal dependence). AI in 2025-2026 is mostly workout drafting, reply drafting and check-in summaries, shipped as add-ons.

### Cited Findings
- Trainerize: habit coaching on the free tier; AI Workout Builder from Grow; custom consultation forms and challenges on Pro; Smart Meal Planner and custom recipes in nutrition add-on/Studio; API access on Studio Plus — [trainerize.com/pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/)
- Trainerize AI: AI Workout Builder entered beta early 2026, ABC says 40%+ of workouts created are AI-generated (self-reported); FitMetrics (Sept 2026) adds AI-assisted check-ins and progress analysis, program and nutrition drafting, bulk client messaging; ABC says coaches handle "2x to 4x" clients (marketing claim) — [ABC Fitness blog, 3 Sep 2026](https://abcfitness.com/?p=30257)
- Everfit Pro: booking, habit coaching, forms, food journal, basic meal plans, advanced program builder, community forums, team management; Studio adds group chat, broadcast, on-demand portal — [everfit.io/pricing](https://everfit.io/pricing/)
- Everfit AI: Smart Response (AI-drafted replies in coach's tone), AI Workout Builder, MacroSnap meal logging, AI meal plans (menu tags, no plan-level detail) — [everfit.io/pricing](https://everfit.io/pricing/)
- Everfit nutrition: manual logging or MyFitnessPal/Cronometer sync; no native food database described; a competitor scores it 4/10; lacks allergy filters and white-label meal-plan PDFs — [Promealplan Everfit review, Oct 2026](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/everfit-review-2026)
- Kahunas feature set: nutrition plans, habit tracking, check-in forms, workout builder, AI insights from the smallest tier; automations, recipes, Zapier/Make from 25 seats; team seats from 50; "large food-tracking database" (size unstated) — [Coachway](https://coachway.io/articles/kahunas-pricing/)
- CoachRx: workouts, nutrition and lifestyle coaching, reusable training cycles and templates, multi-coach business dashboard with compliance and retention metrics — [Capterra CoachRx](https://www.capterra.com/p/253158/CoachRx/pricing/)
- TrueCoach: strongest at 1:1 messaging, simple, no automation (competitor view) — [FitBudd comparison](https://www.fitbudd.com/insights/everfit-vs-trainerize-vs-truecoach?c84a98fb_page=10)
- Trainerize MyFitnessPal sync described as broken on and off for years (competitor/affiliate review) — [aitoolsbakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=5911)

### Inferences
- Depth on phases, supersets and progression rules is not documented in the pages I could retrieve; I cannot rank builders on it.
- Food photo logging exists in Everfit (MacroSnap) and Trainerize FitMetrics (AI meal analysis) per vendor pages; accuracy for Arabic/Gulf dishes is unevidenced.

### Gaps
- Per-platform detail on PDF export, progress-photo comparison, measurements, team role permissions, Garmin/Apple Health: not verified for most platforms.
- Fitr, PT Distinction, My PT Hub, TrueCoach AI features 2025-2026: not retrieved.

## 3. Arabic/RTL, household measures/local foods, coach-defined assessments, decision queue/risk flags

### Takeaway
No global platform evidences Arabic or RTL support. The only RTL signal is an unanswered Trainerize request about Hebrew. Regional Arabic-first entrants exist (Liaqa, Mudrib, Kilow) but are thin or unverified. I found no evidence of household measures, coach-defined assessments/findings, or a coach decision queue in any benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- Trainerize: user request for RTL (Hebrew on Android) dated 14 Sep 2023, 1 vote, no status, no ABC reply; other threads describe an English-only app and ask for multi-language — [ABC ideas portal](https://ideas.abcfitness.com/forums/167887-coach-trainer-abc-trainerize/suggestions/47245526-right-to-left-language-support)
- Everfit: "English only, no French or Spanish UI"; Arabic/RTL not mentioned — [Promealplan](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/everfit-review-2026)
- Kahunas: described as "English-first," languages not listed — [Coachway](https://coachway.io/articles/kahunas-pricing/)
- Liaqa: iOS app claims "fully Arabic and right-to-left ready" for coaches and clients, Apple Health sync, workouts, multiple nutrition plans per client; but the store language field says English only, no ratings shown, last version 8.14.0 on 26 Aug 2025 — [App Store Liaqa](https://apps.apple.com/ly/app/liaqa/id6736395398)
- Mudrib (AI coach, Saudi-dialect Arabic claimed in listing), Kilow (Saudi Arabic weight-loss app), WHOOP added Arabic citing the Gulf as its fastest-growing market — [Kilow/SceneNow](https://scenenow.com/Business/Saudi-Born-Startup-Kilow-Launches-Arabic-Language-Weight-Loss-App), [Renascence on WHOOP](https://www.renascence.io/news/107877/whoop-adds-arabic-to-app-citing-gulf-as-fastest-growing-market); marketing/news snippets, details unverified
- Risk-style features: CoachRx coach dashboard with compliance and retention metrics — [Capterra CoachRx](https://www.capterra.com/p/253158/CoachRx/pricing/); ABC cites an "AI Churn Predictor" in its own press material — [ABC Fitness blog](https://abcfitness.com/?p=30257), only mentioned in passing via search summary.
- Kahunas "Health Score AI" appeared in a Capterra AU snippet; unverified detail — [Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1075469/Kahunas)

### Inferences
- A Gulf or Egyptian coach cannot serve Arabic-only clients on the big platforms with a native RTL client app, on current evidence (absence of evidence, not proof; verify with trial apps).
- Compliance and retention dashboards exist, but a prioritized, explainable coach decision queue with coach-defined assessments and findings appears unserved.
- Household measures (cup, spoon, piece, local bread/rice portions) and local dish databases are not claimed by any benchmark.

### Gaps
- No direct test of each client app in Arabic locale. Hupe/Harder, PT Distinction, TrueCoach, My PT Hub, Fitr and CoachRx language support not retrieved.
- Liaqa's real depth, pricing and traction: unverified.

## 4. Coach complaints

### Takeaway
Per-client pricing and add-on stacking are the most consistent complaint, then post-acquisition Trainerize bugs/billing/support, UI step count, and weak nutrition. Evidence is mostly competitor-authored or secondhand.

### Cited Findings
- Trainerize: most documented post-acquisition bugs, billing errors, MyFitnessPal sync issues; competitors cheaper for equivalent features (affiliate review) — [aitoolsbakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=5911)
- Trainerize Trustpilot 3.5/5 with a "same issues for over a year" complaint; Capterra themes: lag, slow loading, crashes, reliance on stable internet — [Trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/trainerize.com), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/140262/Trainerize/)
- Everfit: a Capterra reviewer left Everfit because it was too expensive per client; Everfit is 4.8/5 on Capterra (376 reviews) — [Capterra Everfit](https://www.capterra.com/p/202837/Everfit/reviews/?page=9), [Promealplan](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/everfit-review-2026)
- Everfit cons (competitor view): nutrition secondary, meal plans a paid add-on, steep per-client pricing, stacking add-ons — [Promealplan](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/everfit-review-2026)
- My PT Hub has documented Trustpilot billing/cancellation complaints — [aitoolsbakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=5901)
- Kahunas: one Capterra UK review from Jan 2026 gave a recommendation rating of 3/10; sentiment mixed — [Capterra UK Kahunas](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1075469/Kahunas)
- Claimed Reddit exit surveys (slow video, broken chat, clunky edits): the Reddit source could not be verified — [aitoolsbakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=5911)

### Inferences
- Pricing fairness at 30-100 clients and fewer paid add-ons are credible selling points, though DAWLYY must still fund its own costs.

### Gaps
- G2, r/personaltraining and Facebook group threads: not retrieved.

## 5. Client complaints (white-label app reviews)

### Takeaway
Thin evidence. Reported client-side issues are speed, crashes and delayed notifications; nothing verified on login friction or offline failures at scale.

### Cited Findings
- Reviewer reports of a slow app, notifications delayed up to 5 minutes, logout when membership lapses, and an app that "will not save"; several reviews dated 2019-2021 — [Capterra Trainerize](https://www.capterra.com/p/140262/Trainerize/), [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/fitness/trainerize-profile/)

### Inferences

### Gaps

## 8. Table stakes DAWLYY must have at launch (inferred from what the benchmarks offer)
- Program builder with templates, reusable cycles, supersets, duplicate across clients, exercise video library.
- Client app with workout logging, messaging, progress photos and measurements, habit tracking, push notifications, reliable offline logging (inference).
- Macro targets and meal plans included, not a paid add-on (benchmarks charge extra; baseline is manual food journal plus MyFitnessPal sync).
- Check-in forms, onboarding/consultation forms, scheduled check-ins.
- Payments and subscriptions (Stripe plus a regional gateway; benchmarks sell these as add-ons).
- Branded client experience (logo and colours) at entry level.
- Apple Health / Google Health sync; PDF export of programs.
- AI-assisted drafting (workout draft, reply draft, check-in summary) with coach approval, since Trainerize and Everfit now ship these.
- Basic assistant/team role.

## 9. Unserved gaps DAWLYY can own, ranked by evidence strength
1. Native Arabic/RTL coach and client experience. Strong: no benchmark evidences Arabic; the Trainerize RTL request is unanswered since 2023; Everfit English-only; Kahunas English-first; WHOOP cites Gulf growth. Caveat: Liaqa claims RTL, and absence needs trial-app confirmation.
2. Pricing and add-on fatigue (per-client plus stacked add-ons, white-label gated). Strong, multi-source but mostly competitor-authored. Bundle nutrition, automation and branding.
3. Nutrition for local food: household measures, Gulf/Levant/Egyptian dishes, Arabic food database. Medium: benchmarks rely on manual logging or MyFitnessPal; Everfit nutrition rated weak; no regional food claim anywhere. No direct evidence of coach demand; validate in interviews.
4. Coach-defined assessments and findings plus a prioritized decision queue with risk flags. Medium-low: only CoachRx compliance dashboards and ABC churn-predictor claims; none described as coach-defined or explainable. This is an absence finding; test with coaches.
5. Affordable true white-label (own store listing). Medium: gated at Enterprise, 200 seats, or add-ons across vendors.
6. Regional payments and currencies (SAR/AED/EGP, local gateways). Low-medium: TrueCoach payments limited to four countries; Kahunas USD-only; others unverified.
7. Client-app reliability (offline, notifications). Low: evidence is old or thin.
