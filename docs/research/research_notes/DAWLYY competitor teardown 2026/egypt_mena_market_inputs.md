# Egypt and MENA market inputs for DAWLYY (as of 7 November 2026)

Method note: about 15 tool calls, mostly search snippets plus two page fetches (CMS Law, Wamda). Most vendor pricing pages were not read directly. Figures marked ESTIMATE come from third-party or vendor-marketing pages and must be re-checked against primary pages before they enter a business case. Where nothing reliable turned up, it is listed under Gaps.

## 1. Market size, pricing, delivery and payment behaviour (Egypt, KSA, UAE)

### Takeaway
No credible 2025-26 figure exists for Egypt online fitness coaching specifically; available numbers are broad category estimates that disagree by definition. Pricing in EGP for online coaching packages was not found in a usable form.

### Cited Findings
- Statista sizes Egypt's broader Health & Wellness Coaching market at US$104.00m in 2024 (fitness, nutrition, meditation apps; wider than coaching) — [Statista Egypt](https://statista.com/outlook/dmo/digital-health/digital-fitness-well-being/health-wellness-coaching/egypt) (ESTIMATE, paywalled snippet)
- Saudi fitness app market: USD 32.5m in 2025 per IMARC; USD 41m (2024 base) per Ken Research. Roughly 25% apart, definitions differ — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/saudi-arabia-fitness-app-market); [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/saudi-arabia-fitness-apps-market) (ESTIMATE)
- Middle East and Africa fitness app market USD 456.32m in 2025, Saudi share about 33.7% of the Middle East segment — [Data Bridge](https://www.databridgemarketresearch.com/reports/middle-east-and-africa-fitness-app-market) (ESTIMATE)
- MEA online fitness expected to exceed USD 1.90bn by 2028, Saudi and UAE leading — [Bonafide Research](https://www.bonafideresearch.com/product/230949786/middle-east-and-africa-online-fitness-market) (ESTIMATE, forecast)
- Egypt-based ISSA-certified trainer lists online program at USD 99 per month — [trainer site](https://anwar.issacertifiedtrainer.com/services-and-rates) (single data point, USD not EGP)
- A Fiverr trainer in Egypt lists USD 30 per hour, gigs USD 100-200 — [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/ahmedhamzax/your-personal-trainer-customizing-workout-and-diet-plan)
- Egyptian coach-software competitor Coachera lists a Pro plan at 250 EGP per month (software fee for coaches, not client price) — [Coachera](https://resume-three-smoky.vercel.app/coachera/marketing) (weak source, a portfolio-style page)
- Gym memberships for context: Gold's Gym Egypt Classic EGP 800 and All Access EGP 1,200 per month — [Subger](https://subger.com/en/th/service/golds-gym-eg) (aggregator)
- Egypt charges 14% VAT, with ETA e-invoicing and a B2C e-receipt mandate effective 15 January 2025 — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/paymob-alternatives) (vendor blog, verify with ETA)

### Inferences
- A USD-quoted offer (USD 99) from an Egyptian online coach suggests coaches price in USD to reach diaspora and Gulf clients; EGP pricing would need local validation by interviewing coaches.
- Category definitions differ so much that DAWLYY should size its market bottom-up (number of coaches times seat price) rather than quote top-down figures.

### Gaps
- UAE standalone fitness app figure: none found.
- Typical EGP and SAR monthly online coaching price: not found in a citable source. Needs coach interviews.
- How coaches deliver (WhatsApp + PDF + Google Sheets) and how clients pay: no source found. Treat as an unverified hypothesis, to be checked in interviews.

## 2. Local and regional competitors beyond FitXpert and Beprime

### Takeaway
Arabic-first coach software exists but is small and thinly documented; FitXpert is the funded incumbent in Egypt. Consumer Arabic coaching apps exist. No store ratings or pricing were collected.

### Cited Findings
- FitXpert: Egypt-based, founded 2023 by Salah Selim and Mostafa Mahmoud; platform for trainers, nutrition centres and clinics (client management, programme design, progress tracking, follow-ups); seven-figure investment from Foras Investment via 0107 Invest; plans Gulf expansion; article gives no user counts or pricing — [Wamda, Jan 2026](https://www.wamda.com/index.php/2026/01/fitxpert-secures-seven-figure-backing-scale-fitness-nutrition-saas-platform)
- ElCoach: Egyptian consumer app, personalized body-transformation programs, targeting Egypt and Saudi, six-figure seed in March 2020 — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2020/03/elcoach-raises-figure-seed)
- Jumpsuite (Cairo, launched 2016) licensed software to trainers and nutritionists; current status unknown — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2016/04/building-global-fitness-platform-streets-cairo)
- Gorilla Fit: Egyptian app by creator Hassan Gabr — [CairoScene](https://cairoscene.com/buzz/gorilla-fit-is-the-new-concept-egyptian-app-making-fitness-accessible)
- Fitness Storm: Saudi Arabic platform with fitness, nutrition, physical therapy and life coaching programs — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lu/app/fitness-storm/id6463420120)
- Mudrib: Arabic-first AI coach with Saudi dialect, USD pricing — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/bw/app/id6761298892)
- Yalla Reyada: Arabic app where trainers upload content and earn — [Catalyst2030](https://catalyst2030.net/organisations/yalla-reyada/)
- FitBudd has a Saudi Arabia coach landing page, so global white-label coach apps are marketing into KSA — [FitBudd](https://fitbudd.com/coaches/saudi-arabia)

### Inferences
- FitXpert's Gulf plan means DAWLYY's KSA entry will meet it. Differentiation has to be product quality and coach workflow, not mere Arabic support.

### Gaps
- Beprime was not researched in this pass (listed by the brief as already known).
- Pricing, user scale and store ratings for all competitors above: not collected. Need direct app-store and pricing-page reads.
- Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, My PT Hub Arabic support: not checked.

## 3. Payments (Paymob, Fawry, Kashier, Geidea, Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, Stripe)

### Takeaway
Paymob is the one Egyptian gateway with documented recurring-billing (tokenized subscriptions). Stripe does not onboard Egyptian-registered entities directly. Fees below are unverified third-party figures.

### Cited Findings
- Paymob Subscriptions: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual and annual cycles, fixed amounts, retries for failed payments, built on tokenized cards — [Paymob developer docs](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/payments-and-features/core-features/subscriptions); [Paymob subscriptions page](https://paymob.com/en/subscriptions)
- Paymob fee quoted as 2.75% by comparison sites; another roundup says 2.5%-2.95% — [PaymentProviders.io](https://paymentproviders.io/country/eg); [Ecosire](https://ecosire.com/ar/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-by-country-2026) (ESTIMATE, no official schedule found)
- Fawry quoted at 2.75% + 3 EGP, described as the largest Egyptian cash-and-cards network — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/paymob-alternatives) (ESTIMATE, vendor blog)
- Paymob is profitable in Egypt and raised USD 22m in 2024 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/11/paymob-lands-another-22-million-and-is-profitable-in-egypt)
- Stripe is not directly available to Egypt-registered businesses; a registered US company is the usual workaround — [Stripe guide for Egyptians](https://foundlie-web.pages.dev/blog/stripe-guide-for-egyptians); [Condia](https://thecondia.com/stripe-africa-payments-tax-2025/) (not an official Stripe page)
- Merchant-of-record route for Egyptian SaaS exists as an alternative — [Dodo Payments MoR guide](https://dodopayments.com/zh/blogs/merchant-of-record-in-egypt) (vendor with a stake)
- dLocal documents Egypt coverage — [dLocal docs](https://docs.dlocal.com/docs/egypt.md)

### Inferences
- Likely first-support set: cards plus wallets through Paymob, plus Fawry cash codes for the unbanked. InstaPay and Vodafone Cash could start as manual transfer proof until an API route is confirmed (unverified).
- Dual-entity thinking: Egyptian clients pay EGP via Paymob; Gulf clients may need a different processor.

### Gaps
- Kashier and Geidea fees, recurring support and API quality: no data found.
- Vodafone Cash and InstaPay merchant APIs, settlement times (T+n), startup KYC lists: not found.
- What coaches actually use today: not found.

## 4. Messaging (SMS OTP, WhatsApp Business API, email)

### Takeaway
WhatsApp utility and authentication messages to Egypt cost fractions of a cent per Meta, but sources disagree on exact rates. SMS OTP to Egypt via global providers is expensive; local aggregators were not priced.

### Cited Findings
- Meta lowered Egypt marketing, utility and authentication rates effective 1 January 2026 — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)
- ChatMaxima (updated July 2026) lists Egypt marketing USD 0.0644, utility 0.0036, authentication 0.0036, plus 14% VAT — [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/egypt/) (ESTIMATE)
- Chatalog (verified against Meta 15 September 2026) lists marketing USD 0.0773, utility 0.0043, authentication 0.0043, Authentication-International 0.0780; service messages billed from 1 October 2026 at utility rate — [Chatalog](https://docs.chatalog.ai/platform-setup/whatsapp-connection-guide/whatsapp-pricing-update) (ESTIMATE; conflicts with ChatMaxima)
- BSP platform fees are separate from Meta rates — [Chakra HQ](https://chakrahq.com/article/whatsapp-api-pricing-guide/)
- Twilio Egypt SMS about USD 0.3959 per message, alphanumeric sender ID needs about three weeks registration; Plivo about USD 0.1091 — [Sent.dm](https://sent.dm/resources/egypt-sms-pricing) (2025 vendor guide; Twilio official page: [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/sms/pricing/eg), figure not read)
- Local domestic SMS rate estimated 0.05-0.10 EGP per message — [Sent.dm](https://sent.dm/resources/egypt-sms-pricing) (ESTIMATE)

### Inferences
- If the Twilio figure holds, an SMS OTP costs up to about 100 times a WhatsApp authentication message. That favours WhatsApp OTP or magic link, with SMS as fallback.

### Gaps
- Unifonic, Vonage, Victory Link, SMS Misr Egypt prices: not found.
- 360dialog, Unifonic WhatsApp BSP markups: not found.
- Opt-in rules and email deliverability providers: not researched.

## 5. Food data (Egypt and Arabic)

### Takeaway
The only official Egyptian table found is the NNI Food Composition Tables (1996, second edition 2006), apparently not available as an open dataset. Open Food Facts is open-licensed but Egyptian coverage is unmeasured.

### Cited Findings
- NNI Food Composition Tables for Egypt, 1996, 115 pages, English; INFOODS also lists an obsolete 1975 table — [FAO INFOODS Egypt](https://www.fao.org/infoods/infoods/tables-and-databases/egypt/en)
- Second NNI edition appeared May 2006 — [FAO INFOODS Egypt](https://www.fao.org/infoods/infoods/tables-and-databases/egypt/en)
- Researchers fill missing items from recipes and other countries' tables (Turkish, Serbian) — [Egyptian Journal of Nutrition](https://ejn.journals.ekb.eg/article_144895.html)
- Open Food Facts is under ODbL, contents under Database Contents License, about 3M products globally, Arabic interface available — [Open Food Facts wiki](https://wiki.openfoodfacts.org/ODBL_License); [DPGA](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/r/open-food-facts)

### Inferences
- ODbL requires attribution and share-alike on derived databases; counsel should check before merging into DAWLYY's proprietary food table.

### Gaps
- Household measures (ملعقة، كوب، رغيف) in NNI tables: not found.
- Licensing and reuse terms for NNI data: not found. Contact NNI.
- Open Food Facts Egypt product count (query `countries:egypt`), licensed Arabic nutrition APIs: not found.

## 7. Legal (Law 151/2020, health data, app stores, disclaimers)

### Takeaway
Executive Regulations were issued 1 November 2025 (Ministerial Decision 816/2025) with prior-authorisation licensing; the planned enforcement date of about 1 November 2026 has arrived. DAWLYY needs a PDPC licence and a cross-border transfer permit if it hosts abroad.

### Cited Findings
- Regulations issued 1 November 2025, published publicly 25 December 2025; one-year grace period, so plan for enforcement from 1 November 2026, with the PDPC possibly treating it as running to end 2026 — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/egypt-s-pdpl-executive-regulations-issued-one-year-compliance-countdown-begins)
- General controller/processor licences, typically three years; supplementary permits for cross-border transfer, electronic direct marketing and surveillance; 90-working-day review; fees depend on activity and data volume — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/egypt-s-pdpl-executive-regulations-issued-one-year-compliance-countdown-begins)
- Transfers abroad, including cloud, need licence or authorisation; recipient protection no lower than Egypt's; adequacy framework and TIA guideline not yet published — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/egypt-s-pdpl-executive-regulations-issued-one-year-compliance-countdown-begins); also [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/01/egypt-regulatory-update-on-data-privacy)
- Sensitive data needs written, Arabic-language consent and heightened record-keeping — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/egypt-s-pdpl-executive-regulations-issued-one-year-compliance-countdown-begins)
- DPO mandatory for legal entities, registered with PDPC; 72-hour breach notice to PDPC, 3 days to individuals — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/egypt-s-pdpl-executive-regulations-issued-one-year-compliance-countdown-begins)
- Other summaries: [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/general/articles/from-policy-to-practice-egypt-issues-executive-regulations-of-the-personal-data-protection-law/); [Kennedys](https://www.kennedyslaw.com/en/thought-leadership/article/2026/egypt-s-personal-data-protection-law-the-compliance-countdown-has-begun)

### Inferences
- Health metrics such as weight, body photos and injuries are likely sensitive data. This is an inference, not stated by CMS.
- Hosting in Egypt, or a licensed transfer, is the compliance question that shapes the hosting region.

### Gaps
- Fines and penalties: not covered by the fetched source.
- Whether the PDPC extended the deadline past 31 October or 1 November 2026: unconfirmed.
- Apple and Google health-adjacent app requirements and medical-disclaimer norms: not researched.

## 8. Infrastructure

### Takeaway
No measured Egypt-to-region latency was found. AWS has a Cairo CloudFront edge. Mobile median speeds are decent but variable.

### Cited Findings
- AWS me-central-1 (UAE) is the second Middle East region after Bahrain; about 17 ms to Bahrain — [AWS blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/now-open-aws-region-in-the-united-arab-emirates-uae); [Economize](https://www.economize.cloud/resources/aws/latency/me-central-1-vs-me-central-1/)
- A 2026 AWS health event reported a power issue in one me-central-1 AZ — [AWS Health](https://health.aws.amazon.com/public/currentevents) (check current status)
- CloudFront edge in Cairo since May 2024 — [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2024/05/new-edge-location-egypt)
- Egypt mobile median download 82.49 Mbps after 5G launch on 4 June 2025 (4G median 46.42) per Ookla mid-2025 — [GSMA](https://www.gsma.com/get-involved/gsma-membership/gsma_resources/5g-helped-egypt-and-tunisia-uplift-mobile-performance-to-new-levels/); 53.06 Mbps May 2026 — [WorldData](https://www.worlddata.info/africa/egypt/telecommunication.php) (conflict; differing periods)
- LTE-or-better coverage about 98% — [WorldData](https://www.worlddata.info/africa/egypt/telecommunication.php) (aggregator)

### Inferences
- With edge in Cairo, a CDN fronting video plus a Gulf or European origin is workable; origin region choice is mostly a legal question.

### Gaps
- Supabase, Vercel and Cloudflare region lists (check their docs); Frankfurt and Bahrain latency from Cairo; data cost per GB in EGP: not found. Measure with real tests.

## Launch implications for DAWLYY

- Payments first: Paymob (cards, wallets, subscriptions via tokens) plus Fawry cash codes. Confirm Kashier and fees by direct quote. Plan InstaPay and Vodafone Cash as manual or later. Stripe only via a foreign entity or merchant of record. All fees here are estimates.
- OTP vs magic link: prefer WhatsApp authentication or email magic link as default, with SMS fallback, since international SMS to Egypt may cost about USD 0.11-0.40 per message (estimate) versus about USD 0.004 for WhatsApp.
- WhatsApp timing: apply for a BSP and Meta verification early (Egypt rate cut effective 1 January 2026). Launch with utility and authentication templates; defer marketing messages (about USD 0.06-0.08, estimate). Remember service messages are billed from 1 October 2026.
- Food DB sourcing: contact NNI about licence and digitisation of the 2006 tables, seed from them, and supplement with Open Food Facts (check ODbL share-alike) and coach-entered dishes with household measures. Budget manual curation of common Egyptian dishes.
- Legal must-dos: register for PDPC licence, apply for cross-border permit if hosting outside Egypt, appoint a DPO, collect written Arabic consent for health data, prepare a 72-hour breach procedure, set up ETA e-invoicing and 14% VAT, and write a medical disclaimer. Confirm enforcement timing with Egyptian counsel.
- Hosting region: choose after counsel decides whether in-Egypt hosting or a licensed transfer is needed; otherwise me-central-1 (UAE) or Bahrain with CDN in Cairo, validated by own latency tests. Design for offline-first logging and adaptive video.
- Positioning: FitXpert is funded and heading to the Gulf; interview 20-30 coaches to validate pricing, delivery habits and glossary before locking scope.
